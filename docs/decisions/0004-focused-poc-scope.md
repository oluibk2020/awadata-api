# ADR 0004: Focused AwaData Commons PoC scope

- Status: Accepted as the current documentation boundary
- Date: 2026-11-04

## Context

The project must demonstrate one complete consent-and-governance access
scenario without becoming a production data platform. A large roadmap would
encourage speculative modules and obscure the central safeguard: both
individual consent and collective governance must permit the same operation.

## Decision

Constrain the current PoC to one synthetic community, one synthetic dataset,
one researcher, one governance representative, one research request, and five
logical areas: community/dataset, consent, research/governance,
deterministic authorization/data access, and audit/benefit commitment.

The governance representative is a simplified demonstration mechanism, not a
claim that a real community protocol has been designed or approved.

## Consequences

- The implementation remains small, understandable, and demonstrable.
- Aggregate academic access, identifiable-access denial, and commercial
  AI-training denial are the acceptance path.
- Multi-community administration, complex governance, financial benefits,
  production integrations, and AI decision-making remain out of scope.
- Future expansion requires an explicit scope decision rather than being
  inferred from the PoC model.
